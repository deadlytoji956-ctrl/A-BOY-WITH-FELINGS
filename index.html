🏫 Adhyay 1
By Tojibiswas

Kaksha ki Smriti

Vidyalaya (School) ka woh aakhri saal aur kaksha ka woh sabse pichla aasan (last bench)—Toji ke liye poori duniya bas yahin tak seemit thi. Priti jab bhi apne mitro ke sath baithkar hasti ya mand-mand muskuraati, toh uske mukh ki woh prasannata Toji ke hriday ko ek ajeeb sa anand de jaati.

Par agle hi kshan, us anand ke piche ek teevra vishaad (grief) chhipa hota tha—yeh dukh ki uski is hansi ka kaaran Toji nahi, balki koi aur tha. Jab saal khatam hua, sabhi chhatra haste hue apne naye jiwan ki taraf badh gaye, par Toji us aakhri bench par apni unhi yaadon ke sath akela maun baitha raha.

“

"Mitro ke sang jab woh haas-parihaas karti thi, 🌸
Uske mukh ki mand-mand muskuraahat mere hriday ko anandit karti thi.
Par agle hi kshan ek tivra vishaad gher leta tha is aatma ko, 💔
Ki uski is prasannata ka kaaran koi aur tha, main nahi."

🕯️ Adhyay 2
Hostel ka Kamra

Hostel ka Kamra

Samay ka pahiya chalta raha, aur Toji naye sheher ke ek hostel ke kamre mein aa gaya. Raat ke andhkaar mein jab hostel ke baaki sabhi chhatra so jaate, tab Toji apne chaladoorvaash (phone) ko uthata aur us nili roshni mein Priti ke purane sandeshon (messages) aur uski chhavi (photo) ko niharta rehta. Uska moha (attachment) itna teevra tha ki woh un smritiyon ko mitaane ka saahas hi nahi juta pata tha.

“

"Mitaane ka prayas toh tivra kiya is kaanpte haath ne, 📱
Par hriday ke is moha se main hamesha kaye liye haar gaya.
Ab toh keval tumhari smritiyon ke saagar mein hi dooba rehta hoon,
Apni hi yaadon ko sansaar bana kar, main is hostel ke kaksh mein jaagta raha." 🍂

"Tum meri woh sabse sundar bhool ho, jise main kabhi sudharna nahi chahta." ❤️‍🩹

🪖 Adhyay 3
Kala aur Kartavya

Kala aur Kartavya

Toji ne apne is dard ko shabdon mein dhaalna shuru kiya. Jab woh manch (stage) par khada hokar apni shayari padhta, toh poora sabhagrah taaliyon ki goonj se bhar jata. Log use ek behtareen kavi samajhte, par koi bhi Toji ke us sachhe dard ko nahi dekh paata tha.

Apne is akelepan aur maut ke bhay se mukt hone ke kaaran, woh desh ki sabse badi guptchar agency RAW (Research and Analysis Wing) mein shamil ho gaya. Ab Toji ek nishthur aur nidar guptchar (secret agent) ban chuka tha, par RAW ke us andhkaar mein bhi, uske dil ka satya nahi badla.

“

"Sansaar ke liye main ek nishthur guptchar hoon mahaan, 🪖
Desh ki raksha mein samarpit hai meri yeh jaan.
Par RAW ke is gupt andhkaar mein bhi ek satya nahi badla,
Is 'Boy with Feelings' ke hriday mein aaj bhi hai keval tumhara sthan." 💔

📜 Upasanhara
Smriti ka Sakshatkaar

Smriti ka Sakshatkaar

Varsho beet gaye. Priti ab apne jeevan mein aage badh chuki thi. Ek din, ek purane pustakalaya (library) mein uski drishti ek gumnam kavita-sangrah par padi, jiska naam tha—"A Boy with Feelings".

Jaise hi usne kaanpte haathon se use khola, use samajh aaya ki desh ka sabse nishthur guptchar Toji, asal mein aaj bhi uski yaadon ka kaidi tha. Priti ke netron se ashru beh nikle, par Toji desh ki gupt vaadiyon mein kahin vileen ho chuka tha.

"Priti ne jab us pustika ke panno ko khola, 📖
Toji ka likha har ek akshar usse mukhrit ho bola.
🎵 'Vekhi javan teri raavan, tethon door na javan...'
Isi Udarian ke sur mein uska dil har pal tha roya.
Woh desh ki raksha mein vileen tha RAW ki vaadiyon mein, 🪖
Par uska hriday aaj bhi bandhi tha keval uski yaadon ki galiyon mein." 🌌
